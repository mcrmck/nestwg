# Code of conduct

NestWG contributors, maintainers, and users must interact respectfully and
professionally in every project space. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
deliberate intimidation, sexualized attention, and publication of another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Critique ideas and evidence rather than
people, assume good faith until evidence indicates otherwise, and make room
for participants with different levels of networking and Go experience.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented to
the affected participant, and handled privately when practical.

Until a dedicated conduct address is published, report conduct concerns
privately to the repository maintainers. Do not use a public issue when doing
so would expose a target or reporter.
